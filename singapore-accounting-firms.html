Delivery workflow

A visible path from source records to reviewer handoff.

01 · SCOPE

Agree the assignment.

Define entity, period, responsibilities, platform, deadlines and expected outputs.

02 · SOURCE

Confirm the records.

Confirm access, source documents and missing information before preparation starts.

03 · PREPARE

Apply your structure.

Complete the agreed accounting work using the firm's conventions and workpaper expectations.

04 · RECONCILE

Connect balances to evidence.

Match material balances to statements, schedules and supporting records.

05 · ESCALATE

Do not guess.

Unusual items, missing evidence and judgement matters are listed for the responsible reviewer.

06 · HANDOFF

Close the loop cleanly.

Deliver completed work, open items, supporting schedules and reviewer queries in an organised pack.
